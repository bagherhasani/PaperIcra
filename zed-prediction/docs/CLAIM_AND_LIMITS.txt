CLAIM AND LIMITS (do not oversell)
=================================

ALLOWED CLAIMS
--------------
1. Hip-steering EKF uses BODY_18 hip heading with fixed gain b=3.
2. On D-walk and straight ZED SVO replays, 1 s ADE is lower for hip-steering
   than CTRV under identical EKF settings (only motion model changes).
3. Numbers (paper table): D-walk 0.192 vs 0.203 m; straight 0.198 vs 0.206 m.
4. System can publish a predicted person disc into Nav2 (engineering prototype).

FORBIDDEN / UNSUPPORTED (as of 2026-09-14)
------------------------------------------
1. "Prediction makes the robot navigate safer / avoid freezing better than now."
   → Block 2 A/B logs do not establish this.
2. Beating González lab ~0.11 m ADE.
3. Multi-scene / multi-person / dense-crowd evaluation.
4. Learning-based SOTA comparison (Social-GAN, Trajectron++, etc.).
5. Claiming 2 s Nav horizon ADE from 1 s eval logs.
